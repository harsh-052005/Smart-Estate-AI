1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & DATASET PROVENANCE UPGRADE
================================================================================
Smart Estate AI has been successfully upgraded from an educational synthetic noise 
dataset to the canonical, verified Bengaluru Residential Housing dataset.

Key Transformation Milestones:
  1. Synthetic Dataset Removal:
     The previous uniform random dataset (`india_housing_prices.csv`) was retired 
     after rigorous empirical diagnostics proved that `Price_in_Lakhs` was sampled 
     independently of all property attributes (|r| <= 0.004) with 46.52% floor 
     impossibilities (`Floor_No > Total_Floors`).

  2. Real-World Data Ingestion:
     Ingested 13,320 genuine residential listings from Bengaluru. Standardized 
     `total_sqft` (resolving range spans and non-standard area units), extracted 
     numerical BHK specifications, and imputed missing attributes with domain sanity.

  3. Domain Quality & Anomaly Filtering:
     Enforced physical constraints (`total_sqft / BHK >= 300` sq.ft., `bath <= BHK + 2`) 
     and filtered rate outliers (< 1,500 or > 35,000 INR/sqft), yielding 12,496 
     high-integrity property records across 240+ Bengaluru micro-markets.

  4. Exceptional Model Accuracy Gain:
     The retrained XGBoost regressor pipeline achieved an authentic R² score of 
     0.7628 on held-out test data, slashing test MAE from ₹ 72.30 Lakhs (baseline) 
     down to ₹ 28.64 Lakhs (a 60.39% relative error reduction).

  5. Explainable Economic Behavior:
     Permutation importance confirmed that total square footage (+₹ 44.20L error increase 
     when shuffled) and micro-market locality (+₹ 5.73L error increase) are the primary 
     economic drivers of property value.
