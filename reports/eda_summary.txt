  6. SURPRISING PATTERNS & REALISM SYNTHESIS
========================================================================
  EVIDENCE-BASED CONCLUSION:
  Do NOT interpret this dataset as verified real-world market data.
  There are STRONG INDICATORS OF SYNTHETIC DATA throughout the entire dataset:

    1. Independence of Fundamentals:
       In real property transactions, Price strongly correlates with Size and BHK.
       Here, r(Price, Size) = -0.003 and r(Price, BHK) = -0.001.

    2. Equalized Subgroup Means:
       Every State, Property Type, BHK category, and construction year averages
       approximately ₹254 Lakhs. Real markets show significant tiering.

    3. Artificial Uniformity:
       Nearby_Schools (1–10) and Nearby_Hospitals (1–10) each have exactly ~10% per bucket.
       BHK (1–5) has exactly ~20% per bucket (spread = 0.16 pp).

    4. Deterministic Identity:
       Age_of_Property is exactly 2025 - Year_Built in all 250,000 rows.

    5. Severe Structural Floor Contradictions:
       116,304 rows (46.52%) have Floor_No > Total_Floors.

  MODELLING IMPLICATIONS:
    - Price_per_SqFt must remain EXCLUDED to prevent severe data leakage.
    - Standard linear regression will find negligible linear signal (near zero R^2)
      unless nonlinear combinations or synthetic generation formulas exist.
    - Any model trained on this dataset cannot be expected to generalize to real housing.
